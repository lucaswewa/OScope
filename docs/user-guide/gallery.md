# Gallery

The projects, and what each holds: acquisitions, results and reports.

## The projects

- **Find** one by part of its name, or by a tag; sort them by when they changed or by name.
- **Each card** shows its picture, its sample, its description and tags, and how much it holds. Its menu renames it, tags it, describes it, sets its sample, or deletes it, asking first.
- **New project** makes one.
- **The keyboard:** Tab reaches the grid; the arrows move between the cards; Enter opens one.

## A project

- **Its records,** by kind. Choose one for its details: its size, frames, camera, objective, calibration and the stage's place; a result's provenance, how it was made and from what; a report's files.
- **Download** any of its files; a download cut short goes on where it stopped.
- **Import files…,** or drop them on the page: TIFFs and the formats the importers read become acquisitions.
- **Export** an acquisition or a result: OME-TIFF, PNG and the other formats offered.
- **Recordings waiting** in scratch can be ingested into a project.

## Viewing an image

*View it, deep-zoomed*: from all of it to its pixels, its tiles coming as you zoom.

- **Zoom** with the wheel or the buttons; *All of it* fits it again. *Full screen*, and Escape.
- **Its look:** the black and white points, gamma, a colour map for one channel, and the plane of a stack.
- **Beside it:** another record, the two moving together on the stage.
- **Save the view** as a PNG, as it is drawn.
