# Jobs

What the instrument is doing, and what waits: captures, scans, stitches, analyses, reports.

- **The queue:** the job running, its progress, and those waiting, in their order. The rail's foot shows the running job too; press it to come here.
- **Reorder** a waiting job by dragging it to its place, or with Alt and the arrows, Home and End.
- **Choose a job** for its detail: its state, its parameters, its log, and its results, each leading to the Gallery.
- **Pause** and **Resume** a job that can wait between its steps, such as a batch.
- **Cancel the job:** a waiting one goes at once; a running one is asked about first. What it made so far stays.
- **The history** lists the jobs ended: completed, failed or cancelled.
- **A job's end** raises a notice; *Show it* comes here.
