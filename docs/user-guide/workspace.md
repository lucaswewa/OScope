# Workspace

Working on what was taken: recipes, analyses, annotations, surfaces, volumes and reports, a view each.

## Recipe

A recipe is a graph of nodes, from the image in to what comes out.

- **Find a node** in the palette, and drag it in or press its *Add*.
- **Join** an output to an input by dragging between their handles, or from the keyboard: Enter on an output, then on an input. Unlike ports are refused as you drag.
- **Choose a node** to set its parameters in the pane on the right. *It can run* says the graph is whole; faults show on the nodes and ports they are at.
- **Live** runs it on the camera's frames, beside the graph, each node's cost on it.
- **The library:** *Open…* OScope's recipes and yours; *Save* and *Save as…* yours; *Import…* and *Export* a recipe's file. *Lay it out* tidies the graph; *Undo* and *Redo* go back and forth.

## Analyses

Choose a recipe, a project and its image, all of it or a region, and *Run*: its result is listed, with how it was made. *Show it* on the image: a count's outlines, each row of its table leading to its cell; a stain's channels, one at a time, and DAB's score. *Compare* two results: their parameters and counts side by side. *Make again* runs one anew.

## Annotations

Choose a project and its image. The tools draw a rectangle, an ellipse, a polygon, a line or a point; *Select* moves, reshapes, names or deletes one. Each is measured as it is drawn: its length, area, centre and the channels' means. *Export as CSV* saves the table.

## Surfaces

A height map, from depth from focus: turned in 3D by dragging, stretched, and coloured by height or by the image. Drag a line across the map beside it for its profile.

## Volumes

A stack in 3D, and another beside it, such as its deconvolution, turned together. Cut into it from each side, and move the three slices through it.

## Reports

Choose a project, tick its results, a template, and PDF or HTML, and *Make it*. The report shows below, as HTML or PDF, its files to download. *Templates*: change one's HTML, preview it, and save it under a name of your own.
