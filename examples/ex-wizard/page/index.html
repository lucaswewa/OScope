ex-wizard

OScope's server, over the simulated instrument, after the calibration wizard has walked its six steps. The terminal prints each step's prompt, what was done, and what the step made.

  • The wizard: its steps, their prompts, and where each stands
  • The sets in use: the geometry, the stage, the dark frame, the flat field and colour
  • Every calibration set
  • The API's documentation: the calibration Thing's wizard_start, wizard_confirm, wizard_skip, wizard_retry and wizard_cancel
